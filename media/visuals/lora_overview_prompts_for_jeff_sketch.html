1. Line Weight, Thickness Modulation & Stroke Dynamics

The authentic jeff_sketch style is defined by its mark-making methodology:

1. Line Hierarchy & Thickness Modulation:
• Primary Boundary Contours (Bold / Heavy): Thick, confident black ink strokes that ground the silhouette and define primary structural boundaries.
• Internal Structural Lines (Medium): Fluid, unbroken single-stroke paths defining internal planes without over-drawing.
• Hairline Accents (Fine / Delicate): Extremely sparse, light pen marks used strictly where necessary.

2. Line Economy ("Less is More"): Forms are resolved with the absolute minimum number of strokes needed. Unbroken, sweeping continuous contours with decisive angles and organic transitions replace cluttered scratching.

3. Negative Space as Active Form: Stark, clean, unshaded white paper. Unfilled white space carries equal compositional weight as the black ink marks.

4. Hatching & Shadowing Discipline:
• Directional Parallel Hatching: Clean, evenly spaced parallel linework for shadow planes—never smudged, smeared, or blended into photorealistic graphite gradients.
• Solid Black Blocking: High-contrast flat black fill blocks placed strategically for structural anchor and graphic punch.
• Unshaded Planes: Lit surfaces and primary feature planes remain pristine white paper with zero internal cross-hatching.

4. Visual-DAGR Architecture (Distillation · Augment · Generation · Review/Refine)

To eliminate single-pass AI prompt compression and achieve 100% reproducible authentic line economy across any foundation model, we operationalize the DAGR runtime architecture from the AETHER/AEC specification (S06_DAGR_METHOD.md) for visual media generation:

Visual-DAGR Stage-by-Stage Operational Map

1. Distill (Deterministic · <0.1ms): Extracts core structure from source lesson JSON (behavioral intent, physical metaphor, anchor entities, format constraints) without LLM calls.
2. Augment (Mechanical Grammar & Staging): Injects mark-making rules (fountain pen ink, zero shading, 40% asymmetric line weight), negative suppression tokens, and multi-tier spatial depth staging.
3. Generate (Base Structural Canvas): Executes the single generative call to establish subject silhouette and composition (Pass 1).
4. Review / Refine (Accountability & Distillation Loops): Chains deterministic image-to-image refinement passes to strip interior clutter (Loop A) and calibrate line-weight asymmetry (Loop B).

Universal Model Portability:

A fresh foundation model session (e.g. Gemini outside this local environment) does not inherently know the fine-tuned token jeff_sketch style. This Visual-DAGR sequence relies purely on explicit mechanical line physics, ensuring 100% reproducible results on any AI model.
